Academic programme

B.Sc. in ICT
International Programme

The four-year programme is taught in English and combines computing foundations with specialised coursework and practical learning.

Areas of specialization

The programme offers eight areas of specialization. Refer to the official curriculum for course choices and completion requirements.

SpecializationArea of study
Computer ScienceAlgorithms, computation and the foundations of software systems.
Database SystemsThe organisation, storage and management of information.
Network and SecurityComputer networks, connected systems and information protection.
Software EngineeringThe design, development and maintenance of software.
Health Information TechnologyApplications of information technology in healthcare.
Interactive Multimedia TechnologyInteractive digital systems and multimedia applications.
Information Systems ManagementThe use and management of information systems in organisations.
Artificial IntelligenceMachine learning and intelligent computing systems.
