International applicants

Admission information

Prospective students from Vietnam should consult the current International Direct Admissions announcement and confirm the requirements that apply to their qualifications.

Application preparation

1. Review requirements

Confirm qualification equivalency, accepted test scores and any subject requirements in the current announcement.

2. Prepare documents

Gather the academic records, test results and supporting documents specified for your application route.

3. Apply through Mahidol

Follow the official application instructions and submit before the stated deadline.

For applicants from Vietnam

Confirm the following with the admissions team before submitting your application.

  • Recognition and equivalency of your Vietnamese high school qualification
  • Accepted English proficiency and other test results
  • Document translation or certification requirements, where applicable
  • Scholarship eligibility through your admission route
  • Visa and accommodation arrangements after admission

Official application information

Application dates, selection procedures and required documents are published by the faculty. Applications must be completed through the official admissions process.
