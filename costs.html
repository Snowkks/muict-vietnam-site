Tuition and scholarships

Published tuition information provides a starting point for planning your studies. Confirm current fees and scholarship eligibility for your admission intake.

International student tuition

Study periodRegular tuition
One regular semester90,000 THB
One academic year · two regular semesters180,000 THB

Annual and four-year amounts are calculated from the published regular-semester rate. Living expenses, travel, visas, insurance, summer fees and other expenses are additional. Fees may change.
